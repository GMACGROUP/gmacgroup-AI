COMPANY OVERVIEW
Gmac Group's tagline is "Connecting talent to opportunity." Its three pillars are Research, Human Capital, and Investment Facilitation. Gmac Group is a research and advisory firm that builds human capital, produces decision-ready evidence, and connects investors to investable projects across Africa. Its geographic focus is centred on West Africa, while virtual programmes reach professionals beyond West Africa, including the African diaspora. The operating model is remote by design. The team has 22 colleagues in five specialist teams working remotely from ten countries. The founder is Raphael S. Ajana. Website: gmac-group.com. This is the Company Profile 2026 edition.

KEY FIGURES
Gmac Group training sessions and impact webinars have recorded more than 2,000 registrations from more than 30 countries. The team works from 10 countries. The research team has doctoral-level quantitative and policy expertise. These figures refer to participants and team composition, not paying clients.

STORY AND VISION
Gmac Group began in response to graduates leaving university without a route into work and institutions making decisions with thin, imported, or outdated evidence. It began with training, coaching, and convening; workshops became series, cohorts, and a community. A research practice then developed under doctoral researchers producing evidence about African markets. Its vision is to build people and evidence at the same time, from inside the continent they concern, to a standard that travels. Founding principle: "Our convenings open the door. The expertise underneath them is what clients hire."
