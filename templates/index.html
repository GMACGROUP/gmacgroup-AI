Gmac Group

Connecting talent to opportunity

West Africa and beyond

Summary

Gmac Group is a research and advisory firm that builds human capital, produces decision-ready evidence, and connects investors to investable projects across Africa.

Practice Areas

  • Applied Research and Policy Consulting Baseline studies, evaluations, labour-market assessments, diagnostics, policy briefs, and feasibility work.
  • Institutional Capacity Building Research methods training, programme design, monitoring and evaluation systems, and capability transfer.
  • Human Capital and Workforce Consulting Graduate recruitment, internship pipelines, talent assessment, employability audits, and market-entry talent strategy.
  • Employability, Events, and Investment Career programmes, signature events, workshops, screened deal flow, investment readiness, and investor matching.

Featured Programmes and Events

  • Leadership 2050 Conference Annual multi-day hybrid convening on leadership, policy, and the future of Africa.
  • Gmac Quarterly Investment Pitch Series Quarterly curated pitch room for investors, funds, and vetted founders.
  • Research and Analysis Series Research methods, certification, AI for research, and emerging-markets programming.
  • Employability Series Career awareness, personal branding, job-market workshops, and structured routes from application to offer.

Who We Serve

  • Institutions Development agencies, companies, investors, ministries, universities, NGOs, and foundations.
  • Individuals Graduates, young professionals, researchers, analysts, senior leaders, and founders.
  • Priority sectors Development finance, financial services, telecommunications, higher education, government, investment, and NGOs.
